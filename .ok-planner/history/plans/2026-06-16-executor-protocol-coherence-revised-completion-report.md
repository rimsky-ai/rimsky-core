# Completion report — executor-protocol-coherence (revised plan)

**Plan:** `plan:2026-06-16-executor-protocol-coherence-revised`
**Spec:** `spec:2026-06-16-executor-protocol-coherence-design`
**Branch:** `feature/nopark`
**Scope evaluated:** uncommitted working-tree diff against HEAD (268 files; +9,863 / −16,366 lines)

The plan refactors the executor protocol surface end to end: the streaming `Execute` collapses to unary with a four-variant `Outcome` oneof, named events fold into terminal-borne `tags`, `attributes_delta` rides every settling terminal, the resume-context channel is removed in favour of attribute carry-forward, three orthogonal dispatch deadlines (`sync_rpc_deadline` / `max_quiet_period` / `max_runtime`) plus a dedicated keepalive endpoint replace heartbeat-keyed liveness, and the async-callback registry persists across supervisor restart. Thirteen TDs, four stories, and the full `.ok-planner/design/` catalog reconcile to the new mechanism.
