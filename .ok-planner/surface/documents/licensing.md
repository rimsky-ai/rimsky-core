# Licensing

## Purpose
An engineer or a compliance reviewer deciding whether they may embed, run, or offer rimsky opens it to learn which surface carries which license and what each license asks of them. They close it able to answer their own case without reading the license texts.

## Covers
- every public published package and the license it ships under
- every public image and the license its contents carry
- the licensed source surfaces the repository declares
