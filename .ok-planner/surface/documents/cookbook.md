# Cookbook

## Purpose
A template author with a problem they recognize opens a recipe to learn which rimsky primitives solve it and in what shape, copies the composed template, and runs it. They close it with a working graph and an understanding of the shape's sharp edges. A newcomer opens a journey and closes it with rimsky deployed and a node driven to terminal. Recipes compose examples; they point at the example for each behavior they use rather than re-showing it.

## Covers
- the recurring problem shapes the public template keys solve, one recipe per shape, each composing the examples it relies on
- the deployment and diagnosis journeys, one per deployment posture and per diagnosis symptom, driven through the public CLI verbs and HTTP routes
- the bundled service images a recipe wires
- the public compose-manifest keys
