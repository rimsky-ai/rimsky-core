# Concepts

## Purpose
A reader new to rimsky's model opens it to learn the vocabulary — node, cascade, frame, claim, run-scope — and where each noun shows up in the surface they will touch. They close it able to read the other documents, and rimsky's own error messages and event log, without guessing what a word means.

## Covers
- the published concepts
- the classes of public surface each concept is expressed through
