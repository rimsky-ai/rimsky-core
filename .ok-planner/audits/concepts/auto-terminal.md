# Held-claim resolution, its deferred cascade, and the two guard shapes it claims

Unsupported, on one clause of one invariant; everything else the concept claims is carried. The resolution entry point does what the body describes and in the stated order: it takes a row lock on the claim handle, refuses to act for a supervisor that does not hold it, refuses a row that is not active, waits while any holder row is still active, waits for the declared holding-subgraph members that have not yet inserted a holder row unless a holder has already failed, waits for expected fan-out children to resolve, then computes the aggregate — any-failed to Abandon, all-completed to Commit, overridden by the parent's snapshotted aggregation policy where children are expected — enqueues the producer verb on the outbox and promotes the handle, all inside the one settlement transaction, with retry and exponential backoff living on the outbox rather than on the disposition. The deferred non-member cascade is real and correctly filtered: each leaf holder transitions to fresh with a success signal or to failed with the uniform abandoned error-class signal, gated on that holder's whole claim portfolio being resolved, poisoned by any abandoned handle it participates in, emitted only to receivers outside the holding subgraph — members having been reached earlier by a member-filtered walk at their own transition into held. The aggregating-parent carve-out is implemented as an explicit check for child runs in the run tree that skips the transition and the deferred cascade entirely, and the racing-writer case is a logged benign skip that leaves the first verdict standing. The poison rule holds through a second mechanism: a held holder settling unsuccessfully marks its own holder row failed and every still-active co-holder row failed in the same transaction before triggering resolution, and a holder still in flight at that moment routes to failed with the abandoned signal when it settles regardless of what its executor returned. The ownership-bail source deletes the row claimant-guarded instead of promoting it, and the pre-dispatch acquire-unavailable path is the single carve-out outside the engine. Twenty-three tests in the resolution suite plus the held-subgraph and durable-lifetime scenarios cover these paths by name. The clause that fails is in the two-guard-shapes invariant: of the three active-row mutations it names as claimant-guarded, promotion and the ownership-bail delete match the holding supervisor in their predicate, but the liveness extension — the only expiry-renewal statement on the handle table in either backend — is keyed on the linked node-run and the active state, never on the acting supervisor, and it is absent from the claimant-guard conformance suite that covers the other guarded operations. The absence-guarded non-active deletions are as described.
