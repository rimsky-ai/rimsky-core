# The service's own warning finding informs without blocking

On the other side of the blocking line, a template carrying a warning-level finding from the same service registered anyway, and the finding came back on the response for the operator to read. A template the validator accepts registered with no findings at all. A service author therefore chooses whether their objection stops the author or merely tells them, and both choices reach the operator through the ordinary registration answer.
