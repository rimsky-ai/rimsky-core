# Rimsky Trademark Policy

"Rimsky" is a trademark of Fall Guy Consulting. This policy explains how the
name and (when one exists) the Rimsky logo may be used by third parties.
The license you receive for the source code (Apache 2.0 or AGPL-3.0-or-later
per the boundaries documented in `COPYRIGHT`) does not include a license to
use the trademarks; use of the trademarks is governed by this document.

## Permitted use

- Referring to the unmodified Rimsky project by name in documentation, blog
  posts, conference talks, comparison articles, and academic publications.
- Identifying a fork or derivative as "based on Rimsky" or "derived from
  Rimsky" in plain prose.
- Describing a third-party implementation, fork, or distribution as
  "Rimsky-compatible" if it passes the upstream conformance suite
  (`rimsky-executor-conformance`, `rimsky-claim-producer-conformance`).
- Linking to the upstream Rimsky repository or website.

## Restricted use

- Forks may not be named "Rimsky" or "Rimsky-<suffix>". Choose a distinct
  project name. Acceptable example: "FooEngine, based on Rimsky."
- The Rimsky logo (when one exists) may not be used by forks, derivative
  projects, hosted services, or third-party tooling without prior written
  permission.
- Hosted services that offer Rimsky-as-a-service to third parties may not
  market themselves under the Rimsky name. "Hosted FooEngine, powered by
  Rimsky" is acceptable; "Hosted Rimsky" is not.
- Use of the Rimsky name in product names, domain names, or company names
  requires prior written permission.

## Conformance branding

A fork or derivative may describe itself as "Rimsky-compatible" if it
passes the upstream conformance suites against the upstream protocol
version it claims to implement. The conformance suites live under
`cmd/rimsky-executor-conformance/` and `cmd/rimsky-claim-producer-conformance/` and
are themselves Apache 2.0; running them against your implementation is
encouraged.

For trademark questions, licensing inquiries, or to request permission for
a use not covered above, contact `licensing@fallguyconsulting.com`.

## Status

This policy lands alongside the first license declaration. A USPTO
trademark filing for "Rimsky" is a separate, deferred process. Until that
filing completes, the name is protected only as a common-law trademark; the
intent stated here is binding.
